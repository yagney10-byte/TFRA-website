// OUR FOUNDATION

Leadership & Mission

Founded with a mission to give high school students hands-on exposure to aerospace design, FEA structural stress modeling, propulsion systems, and real-time telemetric hardware.

DB

Divyansh Bhardwaj

Co-Founder & President

"TFRA was built to bridge the gap between theoretical physics and tangible high-power aerospace engineering. Our goal is to empower every student to design, simulate, build, and test high-altitude systems with industrial rigor."

DP

Dhyan Patel

Co-Founder & VP Engineering

"From aerodynamic CFD passes to PCB soldering for custom telemetry logging, our rockets represent hundreds of hours of collaborative problem-solving. We are pushing the limits of what a high school rocketry team can accomplish."
